      Settings

      gemma3:1b is the default. On a very low-memory computer choose qwen3:0.6b. Run ollama pull <model> once to install it.

      Your data

      Everything is stored in data/memory_seance.db on this computer. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

      Your memories stay on your machine. Open source (MIT). Built with Ollama, SQLite and FastAPI.
